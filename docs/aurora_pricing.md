# Aurora Cloud Storage — Pricing

Aurora Cloud Storage offers three tiers:

- **Starter**: Free. 5 GB storage, 10 GB/month egress, single-region only.
- **Pro**: $12/month per user. 500 GB storage, 1 TB/month egress, multi-region replication, versioning enabled.
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing. Unlimited storage, dedicated support SLA, SSO/SCIM, audit log export.

Overage on the Pro tier is billed at $0.03/GB for storage and $0.05/GB for egress beyond the included quota.
Annual billing gets a 15% discount on Pro and Enterprise. There is no free trial for Enterprise; it requires
a sales conversation and a minimum 12-month contract.
